# MONEY statistical balances

`GET /api/money/v4/statistical-balances` uses the earlier of `asOf` (current time if omitted) and the final PostgreSQL microsecond of the selected `to` date in Asia/Seoul. `accountIds` is an optional comma-separated scope. The account's effective tracking, fund subtype and statistical inclusion at that cutoff determine membership. The ORBIT MCP tool `money_get_statistical_balances` calls the same read with the explicitly requested period, `currency`, `accountIds` and optional `cutoff` (the Web API calls it `asOf`).

| Confidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `VERIFIED` | An eligible observation establishes the balance at the cutoff. Check `source` for bank versus owner evidence. |
| `ESTIMATED` | A dated anchor plus canonical signed movements in `(basisAt, asOfCutoff]` projects a usable balance. A reason identifies gaps in source or statement coverage. |
| `UNKNOWN` | The membership, currency or balance basis is unknown. |
| `CONFLICT` | Same-time observations disagree or independent and ledger figures disagree. The reason and available signed difference accompany the result. A usable latest observation still supplies the displayed estimate. Conflicting observations of equal source priority and time have no uniquely supported amount. |

Provider notifications require a registered, non-excluded canonical leg, compatible amount and direction, verified push format and account provenance. Owner observations and checkpoints are explicitly identified. An owner-attested native statement can establish an exact ending observation and coverage between its bank rows when the rows have second-level precision. Bank PDF coverage is bounded by the declared KST period and the last bank row for an unclosed statement day. The projection applies registered account movements once, excluding opening and reconciliation facts, merged rows and exclusions. The account-specific effective time places each movement in the open-closed interval following its anchor. Missing links, incomplete coverage and uncertain times remain visible as reconciliation reasons. A bank statement opening supports a ledger calculation when no later observation exists. An account with no balance basis has no display amount.

New clients render `displayBalance`, `displayDifference`, `displayTotal`, `verifiedSubtotal` and `estimatedSubtotal` as exact decimal strings or null. `displayable` states whether the member balance can be rendered. `basisAt` and `source` identify the anchor separately from `observedAt`, `bankObservedAt` and `asOfCutoff`. A complete group with at least one estimate has `ESTIMATED` confidence. The additive `displaySubtotal` field is the signed sum of all displayable members, or null when there are none. `partial` is true when it omits a member balance or unresolved membership. Use it with the member list for the KPI, donut and legend. Retain the signs of negative amounts and draw sectors only for positive balances, with their denominator identified. `displayTotal` remains null when any member or relevant membership is unknown or conflicted. `knownCount`, `estimatedCount`, `unknownCount`, `conflicts` and `membershipUnknownCount` explain the coverage. `unknownCount` counts unavailable member amounts. `conflicts` counts conflicting evidence independently. `unresolved[].possibleGroups` identifies which groups an unresolved account can affect.

Legacy `known` and `knownCount` mean verified. Legacy numeric `total` is available only for a complete verified group, and `confirmedPartialSum` contains verified members only. Legacy `balance` and `difference` remain numeric. Exact zero and disambiguated negative balances are valid values. The reads perform no financial writes.
